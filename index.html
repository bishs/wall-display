    Settings
    Drag to preview. 0% = no dimming, 90% = almost dark.
    Tap ▾ on a panel to fold it. Hold a panel for a moment, then drag it onto another panel to swap them.
    App changes show at once. Up to 9 apps.
    To keep the screen on all the time: open Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock, and set it to Never.
    To lock the device to this page: open Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on, then triple-click the top button (or the Home button) while this page shows.
    Auto-Brightness (Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size) sets the screen brightness to match the room.
